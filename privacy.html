Privacy Policy

Bella Zuri Control Room · last updated 19 October 2026

Who this covers

Bella Zuri Control Room is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used by a single person and is not made available to anyone else.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app connects to the owner's own Google Drive so it can:

  • create folders and upload the owner's design files,
  • rename and move those files between the owner's own folders,
  • read the owner's files in order to show small preview images inside the tool.

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. Files stay in the owner's own Google Drive and on the owner's own computer. The app has no server, no database and no analytics. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party, and nothing is sold or shared.

Access tokens

The Google access token is stored only on the owner's computer so the app can keep working between sessions. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party access, which immediately stops the app from reaching Drive.

Other people's data

The app does not collect or process personal data belonging to anyone other than its owner.
