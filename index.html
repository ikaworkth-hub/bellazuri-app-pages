Bella Zuri Control Room

An internal tool used by one person to organise the brand's own design files.

What it does

  • Keeps track of design jobs and the files that belong to each one.
  • Uploads finished artwork to the owner's own Google Drive and keeps the folders tidy.
  • Shows previews of that artwork so the owner can find the right file quickly.

Who uses it

One person — the owner of the Google account it is connected to. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
